    Is the project approved?

    DTCP layout approval and RERA registration are in process. Plots are offered for sale only after RERA registration, and the RERA number will be shown on this page.

    What plot sizes are available?

    Most plots are 30 × 40 (1,200 sq ft): 242 of the 300. Another 35 are deeper plots along the edges, and a few are odd-size. Almost all face east or west.

    When do I get the sale deed?

    On RERA registration. The plot is handed over when the layout is complete, planned for the end of Q1 2028.

    Can I build rental rooms on my plot?

    Yes, within the DTCP building rules for the layout. Many owners near industrial areas build several floors of single rooms for staff and contractors.
