Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short. We collect only what you send us in the enquiry form, we use it to reply to you about The Ridge, and we do not sell it.

1. Who is responsible

Navilu Realty ("Navilu", "we", "us"), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, is responsible for personal information collected through ridge.navilugroup.com. This policy is written with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

2. What we collect

  • Details you give us. When you use the enquiry form: your name, phone number, what you are interested in (buying a plot or the pre-launch investment), your preferred plot size, and anything you write in the message box.
  • Messages and calls. If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive your number or address and the content of your message.
  • Technical data. This website does not set its own cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. The companies that host and serve the website may log technical data such as your IP address, as described in section 5.

3. How the enquiry form works

The form does not send your details to a database on this website. When you press "Send enquiry", it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in as a message to our sales number. The details reach us only when you send that message.

4. How we use your information

  • to reply to your enquiry and share information about The Ridge;
  • to contact you by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email about availability, pricing, approvals and next steps for this project;
  • to keep a record of enquiries and of what was communicated;
  • to meet legal and regulatory requirements.

We use your information on the basis of your consent, which you give when you send us an enquiry. We will tell you about other Navilu projects only if you agree.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • our own sales and customer-care team, and channel partners working on this project under our instructions;
  • service providers that help us run our business, such as customer-relationship and messaging tools;
  • authorities, where the law requires it.

The website also relies on third-party services that receive technical data directly from your browser:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log visitor IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Google Maps shows the embedded location map and may set its own cookies.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries your enquiry message.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy, and some may process data outside India.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as you are in discussion with us about the project and for a reasonable period afterwards, and for longer where the law requires records to be kept. If you ask us to delete your details, we will do so unless the law requires us to keep them.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it;
  • stop contacting you, or withdraw your consent, at any time;
  • record a nominee who may exercise these rights on your behalf.

To make a request, write to vinod@navilugroup.com. To stop marketing messages, you can also reply STOP on WhatsApp or tell the person who calls you.

8. Security

We limit access to enquiry details to people who need them for their work and take reasonable steps to protect them. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about how we handle your personal information, contact our grievance officer:
